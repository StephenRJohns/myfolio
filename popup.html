◆ MyFolio

A cleaner view of your retirement account

A personal-use tool built by an individual developer who wanted a clearer view of their own retirement account. Shared freely in case others find it useful.

  1. Log in to your retirement account at accountview.lpl.com
  2. Click ◆ MyFolio View at the bottom-right
  3. Browse your account's Overview, Holdings, and Activity pages so MyFolio can read the data
  4. Your modern dashboard updates automatically
Personal tool — not affiliated with any brokerage. JJJJJ Enterprises, LLC has no business relationship of any kind with the brokerage firm whose website this tool is compatible with. MyFolio collects no personal data and transmits no personal data to any server. Benchmark charts may fetch public ETF prices from third-party sources. Not investment advice. Verify all figures against your official statements.
