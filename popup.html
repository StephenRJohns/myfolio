◆ MyFolio

A cleaner view for your retirement account
  1. Log in to accountview.lpl.com normally
  2. Click ◆ MyFolio View at the bottom-right of the page
  3. Browse Overview, Holdings, Activity tabs in your LPL account so MyFolio can capture the data
  4. Your modern dashboard updates automatically
Unofficial. MyFolio is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by LPL Financial LLC. All data stays in your browser — nothing is transmitted to any server.
