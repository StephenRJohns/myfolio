### 3.1 Account Data

The Service reads account data from network responses your browser receives from LPL Financial LLC's website. The Service parses these responses based on field names observed at the time of authorship. If LPL changes its API structure, field names, or response shape at any time, the Service may:

- display stale, partial, or incorrect data;
- fail to display data at all;
- attribute data to the wrong account or wrong field;
- silently miss new account types or new transaction types.

**Always verify any figure against your official LPL statement before relying on it for tax filings, financial planning, withdrawals, transfers, or any decision with material consequence.** The Service is no substitute for the authoritative records LPL Financial LLC provides directly.

### 3.2 Benchmark Data

Benchmark comparison data on the Performance tab is fetched from a free public source (stooq.com). This data:

- may be delayed by up to one trading day;
- may contain errors, missing days, or stale prices;
- does not include dividend or distribution reinvestment;
- represents simple price returns, which can differ substantially from total returns for ETFs with significant distributions;
- may become unavailable if stooq.com is offline or changes its data format.

The benchmark figures are **approximate** and intended for **rough visual comparison only**. Do not use them as a definitive measure of any benchmark's performance. For authoritative benchmark performance figures, consult the issuer (e.g., State Street for SPY, Vanguard for VTI, BlackRock for AGG) or a regulated data provider.
