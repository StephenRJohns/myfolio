## 6. Data Sources and Their Reliability

### 6.1 Account Data

MyFolio reads JSON responses that your browser receives from your brokerage's website. The parsing logic is based on field names observed at a specific point in time. The brokerage may change these field names, add new account types, change response formats, deprecate API endpoints, or rename data fields at any time, with no notice to MyFolio. When this happens, MyFolio may continue running and display data that is wrong without flagging the error. **You must not assume MyFolio's data is current or correct.**

### 6.2 Benchmark Data

Benchmark price history is fetched from `stooq.com`, a free public stock-data service. This data:

- may be **delayed by one or more trading days**;
- may contain **errors, missing days, or stale prices**;
- represents **simple price returns** that do not include dividend or distribution reinvestment;
- may differ substantially from **total return** figures that an ETF issuer publishes;
- may become **unavailable** without notice if Stooq changes its service, blocks our requests, or goes offline.

Benchmark figures shown in MyFolio are intended for **rough visual comparison only**. For authoritative benchmark performance figures, consult the issuer (State Street for SPY, Vanguard for VTI, BlackRock for AGG, etc.) or a regulated data provider.
